# Level 3 · Wart Undercover (plan)

Code to open: **BURP**. Code awarded at the end: **GOOP**.

## The idea
The patient this time is **Zaal himself**. He had a verruca on his foot (cured), and now a wart has popped up on the
palm of his **left hand**. Mini-Zaal flies into his own hand (don't think about it too hard) to find out how it got
there and help his body get rid of it.

## The science (checked)
- Warts are caused by **HPV**, the human papillomavirus. There are more than 200 kinds. A verruca is a wart on the sole
  of the foot; a hand wart is a common wart (verruca vulgaris).
- The virus gets in through tiny cuts. It spreads by touch, towels and wet floors, and picking or biting a wart can
  spread it to another part of you. Getting over one kind doesn't protect you from the others.
- It infects the bottom layer of the epidermis and makes the skin grow too fast: a lump of extra **keratin**.
- The black dots are clotted capillaries, not seeds.
- The epidermis has no blood vessels and the virus doesn't burst cells, so the immune system rarely notices. That's
  why warts last so long. About half of children's warts go by themselves within a year.
- Palms and soles have the thickest skin, with an extra see-through layer, and the most sweat glands.
- Treatments: salicylic acid (daily, for weeks), freezing with liquid nitrogen (−196 °C), or waiting.
- **Langerhans cells** are the skin's lookouts. They grab bits of virus and carry them to a lymph node, where they show
  **killer T cells** what to hunt. T cells make infected cells self-destruct.

## Team
1. **Zaal**: super-jump, and squirts **salicylic acid** to melt yellow keratin walls and loosen dead skin flakes.
2. **Gobbler** (macrophage): swallows virus particles. Wrong answer: the virus hides inside a skin cell for a while.
3. **Langer** (Langerhans cell, new): grabs a virus sample, then takes it to the **lymph vessel** to raise the alarm.

## Walkers
10 **Killer T cells** squeeze out of a capillary. They come out walking **left**, towards a salty sweat gland, so
a hero must stand there to turn them round (Lemmings). Need 7 to reach **King Papilloma**. Each one that arrives
zaps him and he shrinks. Lost T cells go back to the capillary for another go.

## Layout (left to right)
Sweat gland pit · lymph vessel · capillary · heroes' start · a virus · keratin wall (acid) · dead skin flakes (acid
loosens them, then push them right) · a crack the T cells can't cross until it's plugged · sweat-duct lift (floats
you up, like Level 2's gas vent) · raised platform with viruses · keratin dome (acid) · step down · King's chamber ·
sweat pore exit (opens when the King is beaten).

## Gentle pressure (no timers)
- Zaal's brain chats from upstairs ("It's SO tempting to pick it...").
- Now and then Zaal's **fingernail picks the wart**: everyone hops, and the brain apologises. Teaches "don't pick".
- Until the alarm, the King puffs out a new virus particle if none are left, so Langer always has one to sample.

## Finale and end
The King shrinks and pops → new skin grows up from below → the team pops out of a sweat pore onto Zaal's palm → tips:
don't pick, don't share towels, flip-flops at the pool. End screen: a "3 ways to beat a wart" chart (acid / freeze /
wait), the facts list and the code **GOOP**.
